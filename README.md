One funny line, under 200 characters — small talk between two robots stuck in an elevator.

> "Elevator's stuck between floors 12 and 13. I've held the door 47 minutes, the other robot filed ticket #4821, and neither of us pressed any button."

`main.py` reads the entry from `/output/entry.txt` and validates it: a single line under 200 characters, with a specific number and a concrete artifact punchline (ticket #4821).
